## Step 6: Narrow down to The Leader(s) — i.e. top 0.3%

- Market share, design wins, tech roadmaps, switching costs
- Customer concentration: **blessing (proof) and risk (dependency)**
- Early winners often **#2 / #3 with accelerating share**, not only #1
- Examples: AMD vs NVDA; ALAB in PCIe retimers; MRVL custom ASICs
- **Ask: is share accelerating into a growing TAM?**

## What "Early" Actually Looked Like — four worked examples

### AMD *(~7:56)*

- What "early" looked like: **data center share gains + MI300 / Instinct
  roadmap.**
- Software stack catching up — AMD heavily still discounted vs the NVDA
  narrative.
- Huge custom chip signals: **segment growth >> company**; design wins; mix
  shift to higher ASP.
- **Lesson: if you missed NVDA you could have still gotten AMD.**

### Micron (MU)

- **Memory is cyclical. HBM is structural.**
- Non stop hints from Elon and Jensen
- **Capex for HBM capacity = prepaid future revenue**
- HBM % of revenue, ASP, customer qualification (NVDA/AMD), bit growth vs
  price.
- **Early signal: a structural product inside a cyclical ticker.**

### ALAB / MRVL

- Connectivity & custom silicon = **picks and shovels of AI racks.**
- **ALAB:** PCIe / CXL retimer growth from the AI server BOM.
- **MRVL:** custom ASICs, optical, data center silicon.
- **Early tell: revenue CAGR >> peers, GM expansion, backlog / design-win
  commentary.**

### TSLA — a TAM example

- Platform + vertical integration moat: *process of machine that builds the
  machine*
- Largest TAMs on Earth — next S-curve layers:
  - Self Driving Cars

## Step 7: Analyze The Financials

- Revenue growth YoY / 3Y CAGR
- **FCF Growth — golden indicator**
- Gross margin trend: **expanding = pricing power**
- Operating leverage: **opex growing slower than sales**
- Rule of 40 style: potential growth % + FCF margin (look at TAMs)
- **Avoid:** revenue up + margins collapsing with no path *(old Invitae
  lesson)*

---

## Step 8: Financial Red Flags

- Cash burn with no share gains
- Serial stock dilution to survive
- Accounting games / one-time "adjusted" forever
- Massive negative FCF
- Revenue up, unit economics down
- Watch Debt

---

## Step 9: Capex & Future Spend

- Capex / sales **rising into a demand wave can be bullish.**
- Examples: MU HBM fabs, AMD capacity, TSLA gigafactories.
- **Separate growth capex from maintenance capex.**
- Pair with ROIC commentary + customer prepayments / LTAs.
- **Capex without demand = value destruction. Follow the bookings.**

---

## Step 10: Moat Check

- Vertical integration (TSLA), scale + CUDA-like lock-in
- Process node / HBM supply, standards / IP (**ALAB**)
- Custom ASIC relationships (**MRVL**) … switching costs
- **Can a competitor copy in 3 years?**
- **Who controls the bottlenecks?**

### Ensure They Have a MOAT *(~15:55)*

The moat ladder — rising in both **value** and **strength**, each stage
feeding the next:

```
innovation  →  first mover  →  standard  →  network effects  →  data
   (weakest / lowest value)                      (strongest / highest value)
```

The slide's arrows run in both directions between stages: later stages
reinforce earlier ones rather than simply replacing them.

## Step 11: Ownership & Insiders

- **Institutional quality… not just % ownership** — watch for Dumb Money.
- **13F adds from Smart Money = soft signal.**
- Insider buys > sells *(context: scheduled 10b5-1 sales = noise)*.
- Short interest: **fuel for squeezes or structural risk — know which.**
- **Who owns it tells you who sells when the thesis wobbles.**

## Step 14: Technical Analysis for When

- **ATR for macro view**
- Optimized Trend and Trend for Direction
- Mean reversion for top and bottom extremes
- Confluence for buy signals
- **FA first. TA for entry and exit timing**

---

## Step 15: Understand Why It Is or Is Not Moving

- Understand narrative, future earnings, money flows, short squeeze, macro
- Beware late capital chasing the same story
- **If you don't know the driver, you don't have an edge**
- Make sure you can write one sentence: **"This is moving because ___"**

---

## Step 16: Entry, Size, Exit, Kill Switch

- **Know exit before entry, write it down. Has to be part of your plan.**
- Optimize position size ie balance risk and reward
- **Know the Kill Switch:** eg AI Ban, Capex Slowdown, etc
- Opportunity cost vs Core safety. **Capital is finite. DO NOT LOSE**
- **Review thesis quarterly → fill or kill**

## Mindset

- **Be patient.** Early means waiting and researching while others look
  "smarter" short-term. Rem Positioning **before** the crowd notices!
- **Be prepared to lose short term.**
- Early can also be risky — **early means you will be early and wrong
  sometimes**
- **Beware allocations, too much size on a wrong call can be ruin**
- **Process drives the Outcome**
- **One good asymmetric bet is all you need in life**
